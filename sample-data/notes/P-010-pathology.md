> SYNTHETIC PATHOLOGY REPORT – fictional patient P-010 – for hackathon use only.

# Pathology report – biopsy rectum – 7 Oct 2025

**Specimen:** Endoscopic biopsies, mid rectum, 8 cm from the anal verge.

**Microscopy:** Fragments of moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma infiltrating the lamina
propria. No signet ring cell component.

**Immunohistochemistry / molecular:** MMR proteins retained (MLH1, PMS2, MSH2, MSH6) – proficient
MMR. RAS wild-type, BRAF wild-type.

**Conclusion:** Adenocarcinoma of the rectum, grade 2, pMMR, RAS/BRAF wild-type.

# Re-biopsy of the scar – 5 Mar 2026

**Specimen:** Three biopsies from a flat white scar at the former tumour site.

**Microscopy:** Fibrosis and chronic inflammation with reactive epithelial changes. No vital tumour
cells identified in the material examined.

**Comment:** Negative biopsies of a scar cannot exclude residual tumour deeper in the wall; the
findings must be read together with MRI and endoscopy.
