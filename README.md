# Health Rewired · Oncology Hackathon 2026 · Munich

Turn your oncology idea into a working prototype. You don't need to write any code.

**Live starter app:** https://healthrewired-munich.icywater-8235048a.westeurope.azurecontainerapps.io

## How it works

```text
YOUR IDEA  →  CONVERSATION WITH AN AI COACH  →  BETTER IDEA  →  AI BUILDS IT  →  LIVE LINK
```

1. **Share your idea** as a GitHub issue (see below).
2. **Talk with the coach.** Within a few minutes, an AI coach replies in the issue with what's
   promising, what's unclear, and questions for you. Answer by **editing the issue** or
   **replying with a comment**. The coach reads it again every time.
3. **Get approved.** When the idea is ready, the coach posts a **🚀 Implementation proposal**
   describing what will be built. GitHub Copilot then starts building.
4. **Open your prototype.** When the first version is ready, a **live link** appears in your
   issue. Every improvement updates the same link.
5. **Ask for changes.** Open the pull request linked in your issue and write a comment starting
   with `@copilot`, for example: `@copilot make the missing results stand out in red`.

## Share your idea

1. Go to **[Issues → New issue](../../issues/new/choose)** and choose **💡 Oncology idea**.
2. Answer the questions in your own words. Plain language is best.
3. Click **Create**. That's it. The coach will reply in the issue.

## Things to remember

- 🚫 **Never use real patient data.** Only use made-up (synthetic) examples.
- 🎯 **It must be about oncology.** Other ideas get friendly suggestions to reframe them.
- 🧠 **Your clinical insight is the point.** Describe the real moment in your day where things
  go wrong, and who is affected. That's what makes an idea worth building.
- 🚀 **Think beyond a chatbot.** The best ideas have AI that *does* something: it notices,
  checks, prepares or reconciles. A summary alone isn't enough.
- 🧑‍⚕️ **Say what stays human.** Decide which decisions must always be made by a person.
- 💬 **It's a conversation.** Improving your idea over a few replies is normal and expected.
  Nothing gets rejected for good. You can always edit and try again.
- 🧪 **It's a prototype**, not a medical device. It's for exploring ideas, not for clinical use.
